## Platform

web

## Users

Mac developers, AI engineers running overnight workflows, audio producers, remote workers, and power users who need their MacBook to stay alive and working with the lid shut without being tethered to an external monitor or dealing with sleep-assertion deadlocks.

## Product Purpose

lidZen bypasses macOS clamshell limits so background jobs, AI coding agents, builds, and audio streams keep running uninterrupted with the lid closed. It includes deep kernel-level path diagnostics to identify and non-destructively suspend (Mach task_suspend / SIGSTOP) runaway processes that drain battery and overheat laptops.

## Positioning

Unlike legacy clamshell tools that rely on kernel extensions (kexts) or forced sleep overrides that overheat laptops, lidZen is notarized by Apple, runs 100% in user space (zero kexts), weighs 18 MB, and pairs clamshell continuity with diagnostic telemetry so users know their Mac stays cool and efficient while working lid shut.
